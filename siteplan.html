Website Planning Document

Site Name

Summit Studio

The name Summit Studio represents reaching professional and personal peaks. It reflects a clean, modern brand focused on helping entrepreneurs and professionals present their best image online.

Site Purpose

This website provides information about professional photography services including executive headshots and modern portrait sessions. The site helps entrepreneurs and business professionals understand available packages, view sample work, and contact the studio to book sessions.

Scenarios

  • How do I choose the right headshot package for my role (startup founder, corporate professional, or small business owner)?
  • What should I wear and how should I prepare for a professional headshot session to look confident and polished?

Color Scheme

  • Charcoal Gray (#2E2E2E) - Used for headings, navigation text, and footer background.
  • Soft Off-White (#F5F5F5) - Used for page backgrounds and content sections to keep the design clean and professional.

Typography

  • Arial - Used for all headings and body text for a clean, professional, readable layout.
